project7III Screenshots is a SwiftUI package that automates App Store screenshot generation. It renders production views within Apple device frames, supports multiple locales and device sizes, and exports App Store-ready images directly from your test suite.  

We use it for all our app screenshots at project7III, both for the App Store and our website. It makes updating existing screenshots and creating new ones quick and easy.  

It's open source, and contributions are very welcome. Check it out:  
→ [project7III Screenshots](https://github.com/nilsmango/project7III-Screenshots)
